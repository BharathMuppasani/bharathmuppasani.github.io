Heuristic Design

A Useful Heuristic Is a Cheap Lower Bound on the Remaining Delivery Work

For this warehouse problem, a simple heuristic can approximate how much delivery work is left without solving the entire plan exactly.

Design idea

Count unfinished transport, not exact future choices.

We estimate the remaining cost using shortest-path travel for the deliveries that still need to happen.

Delete Relaxation Heuristic

Ignore negative effects ($h^+$).

Classic heuristics like h-add or FF assume achieved facts are never deleted. By ignoring the capacity limit, the robot hypothetically solves the rest of the problem instantly, creating a fast polynomial-time lower bound estimate.

Why it is still useful

The estimate decreases when the right subgoals are achieved.

Picking A, delivering A to the lab, and finally carrying B to the office all reduce the remaining lower bound.
