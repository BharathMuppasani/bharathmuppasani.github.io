Optimization View

Planning Can Be Written as a Constraint Optimization Problem

A plan is a sequence of decision variables constrained by the transition model. Optimization chooses the best feasible sequence under a chosen metric.

Canonical finite-horizon formulation
min a0 , a1 , … , aH-1 ∑ t=0 H-1 c ( st , at )
Initial
s0 = I. The first symbolic state must match the modeled warehouse start configuration.
Dynamics
s(t+1) = T(s(t), a(t)). Actions change the state only according to the planning model.
Validity
a(t) in A(s(t)). Pick, drop, and move are legal only when their preconditions hold.
Goal
sH |= G. The terminal state must satisfy (A at lab) and (B at office).
Planning language to optimization language
same problem, different lens

Nothing mystical happened here. Planning is trajectory optimization with logical dynamics and feasibility constraints.
