Motivation

Why Is Planning Needed?

Planning is needed when good decisions are not isolated. One action changes the state, the remaining constraints, and the quality of every action that comes next.

Future Feasibility Changes

Moving to storage makes Pick(B) feasible. Moving to the lab too early may leave both packages undelivered.

Costs Accumulate Over a Sequence

Travel time, distance, energy, lateness, and risk are incurred over the whole trajectory, not on one step alone.

Goals Require Coordination Over Time

“A in lab and B in office” cannot be checked after one move. It requires an action sequence that keeps the task feasible throughout.

Robotics

Navigation, manipulation, and task sequencing under geometry and resource constraints.

Logistics

Warehouse routing, pickup-delivery, fleet scheduling, and order fulfillment.

Autonomy

Vehicles and agents that must act over time, not just solve one-shot choices.

Operations

Sequential resource allocation where today’s action changes tomorrow’s feasible set.

Optimization hook: once a task is sequential, the next question is not just “is there a plan?” but “which feasible plan best trades off distance, time, energy, or risk?”
