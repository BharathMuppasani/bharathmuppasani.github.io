Summary

Takeaways

Planning is structured search.

We search over implicitly generated symbolic states, not just over explicit graph nodes.

PDDL gives the search space meaning.

Predicates, actions, preconditions, and effects define which trajectories are feasible.

Optimization decides what “best” means.

Action count, travel cost, energy, and risk can all lead to different optimal plans.

Heuristics are the scalability lever.

Useful estimates of remaining effort dramatically reduce the amount of search required.

Dynamics motivate learning.

When models drift, execution becomes uncertain, or reactions must be reused, learning becomes a natural extension of planning.

Lecture Arc
Motivation → Search → Planning → Optimization → Dynamics → Learning

Use planning when a trusted model exists. Use learning when the model must be estimated, adapted, or replaced by a reusable policy.
