Metric-Sensitive Optimality

The Optimal Plan Depends on Which Cost We Choose to Minimize

all reach A to lab and B to office
Plan Route idea Travel cost Energy Comment
A Direct delivery first, little detour 8 11 Fastest route, most energy-hungry.

If the objective is minimum travel cost, Plan A is optimal.
Travel cost versus energy

Optimality is objective-dependent. A planner is not asked for “the best plan” in the abstract. It is asked for the best plan under a specific metric or constraint set.
